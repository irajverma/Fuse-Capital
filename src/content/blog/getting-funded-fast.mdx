## Introduction

Getting funded quickly doesn't have to be complicated. Whether you need capital to cover a gap in cash flow, seize a time-sensitive opportunity, or simply keep operations running smoothly, there are several fast-track financing options available to business owners today.

## Know What Lenders Are Looking For

Speed of approval depends largely on how prepared you are. Most alternative lenders focus on:

- **Monthly revenue**: consistent bank deposits signal repayment capacity
- **Time in business**: typically at least 6 months
- **Bank statements**: the last 3 months is usually enough to start

Having these ready before you apply cuts processing time dramatically.

## Fastest Options to Consider

### Merchant Cash Advances

MCAs can fund in as little as 24–48 hours. Repayment is taken as a percentage of daily sales, making them flexible during slower periods.

### Business Lines of Credit

Once approved, a line of credit lets you draw funds on demand: no reapplication needed. Great for recurring needs.

### Short-Term Term Loans

Fixed amounts with fixed repayment schedules. Many alternative lenders offer same-day or next-day decisions.

## Final Thoughts

The fastest path to funding is the one where you arrive prepared. Know your numbers, have your documents ready, and work with a team that understands your industry.
